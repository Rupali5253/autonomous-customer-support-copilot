1. Purpose and Acceptance
These Terms and Conditions govern the general use of NovaCart services, the purchase of products, and
interactions between NovaCart and its customers.
By creating an account, browsing the platform, placing an order, or using NovaCart services, the customer agrees to
follow the applicable terms. Product-specific terms, policies, and legally required notices may apply in addition to
these general terms.
2. Eligibility
Customers must provide accurate information and use the service for lawful purposes. Where an account is
required, the customer is responsible for maintaining the accuracy of account information.
NovaCart may restrict or suspend access when required to protect customers, the platform, or the integrity of
transactions.
3. Account Responsibilities
Customers are responsible for:
 Providing accurate account and delivery information.
 
 Keeping account credentials confidential.
 
 Reviewing order details before confirmation.
 
 Reporting suspicious or unauthorized account activity.
 
 Using the platform only for lawful purposes.
 
Customers should never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or confidential authentication information with support
agents or the AI assistant.
4. Product Information
NovaCart aims to provide accurate product descriptions, images, specifications, availability, and pricing. However,
information may occasionally require correction or updating.
The latest product listing and applicable order information should be treated as the primary source for a customer's
purchase. If important information conflicts across sources, the issue should be reviewed rather than resolved by
assumption.

5. Pricing and Taxes
The price presented during checkout is the applicable order price, subject to clearly displayed taxes, shipping
charges, discounts, and other conditions.
NovaCart may correct pricing or listing errors where appropriate. If a material pricing error affects an order, the
customer may be contacted before fulfillment continues.
6. Orders and Order Acceptance
Submitting an order constitutes a request to purchase the selected products. An order becomes confirmed when
NovaCart's systems successfully accept and record the order.
NovaCart may decline or cancel an order in limited circumstances such as inventory unavailability, payment failure,
suspected fraudulent activity, operational restrictions, or material listing errors.
7. Payment Terms
Customers must use an available and authorized payment method. Payment information is processed through
supported payment providers and applicable security controls.
A successful payment attempt does not by itself guarantee that an order has been confirmed. If money is deducted
but an order is not confirmed, the payment/order reconciliation process should be followed.
8. Shipping and Delivery
Delivery estimates are provided according to available fulfillment and logistics information. Actual delivery may vary
because of operational, transportation, weather, location, or other unforeseen conditions.
The Shipping & Delivery Policy provides detailed rules for tracking, delays, failed delivery, damaged shipments, and
missing packages.
9. Cancellation
Customers may request cancellation while the order remains in an eligible cancellable stage. Once an order
reaches a non-cancellable fulfillment or shipment stage, standard cancellation may no longer be available.
The NovaCart Cancellation Policy provides the detailed cancellation rules.
10. Returns and Refunds
Eligible returns and refunds are subject to the applicable product conditions and policies.
The Return Policy defines return eligibility and process. The Refund Policy defines refund handling and status. A
return or cancellation does not automatically mean that a refund has already been completed.
11. Prohibited Use
Customers must not use NovaCart services to:
 Commit fraud or abuse.
 
 Attempt unauthorized access.
 
 Interfere with platform operations.
 
 Submit intentionally false claims.
 
 Abuse promotional programs.
 
 Use another person's account without authorization.
 
 Engage in unlawful activities.
 
NovaCart may investigate and restrict activity that violates these requirements.
12. Reviews and Customer Content
Where NovaCart permits reviews, feedback, or other customer-submitted content, customers should provide honest
and relevant information.
Content must not contain unlawful material, threats, confidential information belonging to another person, or
intentionally misleading claims. NovaCart may moderate content according to applicable platform rules.
13. Promotions and Offers
Promotional offers may have separate eligibility requirements, validity periods, product restrictions, usage limits, or
payment-method conditions.
Customers should review the specific offer terms. A promotion should not be treated as universally applicable to
every product or customer.
14. Privacy and Personal Information
NovaCart should collect and process customer information only for legitimate service, order, support, security, and
operational purposes in accordance with the applicable privacy notice.
Customers should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information with support. The AI assistant must not request
passwords, OTPs, PINs, or confidential payment credentials.
15. Service Availability
NovaCart aims to maintain reliable service but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability. Temporary outages,
maintenance, technical failures, third-party service interruptions, or other operational events may affect platform
availability.
Customers should retry later or contact support when a service issue prevents completion of an important
transaction.
16. Limitation of Automated Support
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot is intended to provide assistance based on approved NovaCart
knowledge and available system information.
The AI assistant should not invent policy exceptions, guarantees, refund confirmations, delivery commitments, or
account decisions. When information is insufficient or an issue requires internal investigation, the assistant should
escalate the case.
17. Customer Support and Escalation
General informational questions can normally be answered without creating a ticket.
A ticket or human escalation may be appropriate for payment reconciliation, disputed refunds, complex returns,
missing shipments, account-security concerns, contradictory order records, or other cases requiring internal
verification.
The Customer Support & Escalation Policy defines the detailed escalation rules.
18. Changes to These Terms

NovaCart may update these Terms and Conditions when business, operational, legal, or service requirements
change.
The latest published version should be used when answering customer questions. Historical orders may remain
subject to terms applicable at the time of the transaction where required.
19. Governing and Legal Requirements

20. Examples
Example 1 — Customer asks: “Do I have to agree to terms before ordering?”
Expected handling: Explain that placing an order requires acceptance of the applicable terms and policies.
Example 2 — Customer asks: “Can support ask me for my OTP?”
Expected handling: No. The customer should never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or confidential authentication
information.
Example 3 — Customer asks: “The product price changed after I ordered. Which price applies?”
Expected handling: Explain that the confirmed order price is generally the relevant order price, subject to applicable
terms and any documented pricing error.
Example 4 — Customer asks: “Can AI guarantee my refund today?”
Expected handling: No. The assistant should only provide a confirmed refund status when supported by system
information and should avoid unsupported guarantees.
