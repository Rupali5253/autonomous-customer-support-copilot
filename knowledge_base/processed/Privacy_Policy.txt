1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how NovaCart collects, uses, protects, stores, and handles information associated with
customers and users of its services.
NovaCart is a fictional company created for the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot project. This document is a
project knowledge-base policy and is not legal advice or a substitute for a real company's legally reviewed privacy
notice.
2. Scope
This policy applies to information processed through NovaCart's customer-facing services, including account
activity, product orders, payments-related support information, delivery information, customer support interactions,
tickets, feedback, and AI-assisted support conversations.
Specific products or services may have additional privacy notices where required.
3. Information We May Collect
Depending on the service used, NovaCart may collect:
 Name and contact information.
 
 Email address and mobile number.
 
 Account information.
 
 Delivery and shipping information.
 
 Order details.
 
 Product and purchase information.
 
 Payment status and transaction references.
 
 Customer support tickets and messages.
 
 Feedback and ratings.
 
 Technical information required to operate and secure the service.
 
 Information voluntarily provided through support attachments or screenshots.
 
4. Information We Do Not Ask Customers to Share
NovaCart support agents and the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot must not ask customers to provide
passwords, OTPs, PINs, authentication codes, or complete confidential payment credentials.
If such information is accidentally shared, the support system should avoid repeating it and should guide the
customer toward a secure process.
5. How We Use Information
NovaCart may use customer information to:
 Create and manage customer accounts.
 
 Process and fulfill orders.
 
 Provide shipping and delivery services.
 
 Handle payments and transaction reconciliation.
 
 Process returns, cancellations, and refunds.
 
 Provide customer support.
 
 Create and manage support tickets.
 
 Detect and investigate fraud or security issues.
 
 Improve service quality and system reliability.
 
 Measure customer-support performance.
 
 Comply with applicable legal and operational requirements.
 
6. AI-Assisted Customer Support
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot may process customer messages to understand intent, retrieve
relevant approved knowledge, generate responses, create tickets, and determine when human escalation is
necessary.
The AI assistant should use only the information needed to handle the customer's request and should not request
unnecessary sensitive information.
7. Knowledge Base and RAG
The support system may use approved NovaCart policy documents as a knowledge base for retrieval-augmented
generation.
Knowledge-base documents are intended to provide policy information to the AI assistant. Customer personal
information should not be added to policy documents simply for the purpose of answering general questions.
8. Customer Support Conversations
Customer messages may be processed to provide support and maintain a record of support interactions.
Where a ticket is created, relevant information may be stored with the ticket so that human agents can understand
and resolve the issue.
9. Ticket Information

A support ticket may contain:
 Customer identifier.
 
 Intent.
 
 Subject.
 
 Customer message.
 
 Priority.
 
 Status.
 
 Relevant order or transaction reference.
 
 Escalation reason.
 
 Resolution information.
 
Only information reasonably necessary for support should be included.
10. Payment Information
NovaCart may process payment status and transaction references required for order and support operations.
The customer-support system should not request or store complete payment-card credentials through ordinary chat.
Payment processing may be handled by authorized payment providers and systems appropriate for the transaction.
11. Cookies and Technical Information
NovaCart may use cookies or similar technologies required for authentication, preferences, security, analytics, or
service functionality.
Technical information may include browser type, device information, approximate usage information, error
information, and security-related events where necessary to operate and protect the service.
12. Sharing of Information
Customer information may be shared with authorized service providers or partners when necessary to provide
services such as payment processing, delivery, hosting, customer support, security, or other operational functions.
Information should be shared only for legitimate purposes and according to applicable agreements and
requirements.
13. Legal and Security Disclosure
NovaCart may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable legal requirements,
respond to lawful requests, protect customers or the service, investigate fraud, or prevent serious security or
operational abuse.
14. Data Security
NovaCart should use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against
unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.
Security measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, secure
development practices, and restricted internal access.
15. Employee and Agent Access

Customer information should be accessible only to authorized personnel who need it to perform their
responsibilities.
Support agents should access customer information only for legitimate support, verification, investigation, or
operational purposes.
16. Data Retention
NovaCart should retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it
was collected, operational requirements, dispute handling, security, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
Different types of information may have different retention periods.
17. Deletion and Account Closure
Customers may request account closure or deletion where applicable.
Some information may need to be retained for legitimate purposes such as transaction records, security
investigations, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal requirements.
18. Customer Privacy Rights
Subject to applicable law and operational requirements, customers may have rights concerning their personal
information, such as:
 Requesting access to information.
 
 Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
 
 Requesting deletion where applicable.
 
 Asking about how information is used.
 
 Raising a privacy concern or complaint.
 
Requests should be directed to the appropriate privacy contact.
19. Privacy Requests and Support Tickets
A privacy-related request may require human review because identity verification or specialized processing may be
necessary.
The AI assistant should not independently promise that information will be deleted, corrected, exported, or disclosed
unless the applicable process confirms that action.
20. Children's Information
NovaCart services are not designed to intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from children.
Where age-related restrictions or parental requirements apply, the applicable service terms and legal requirements
should be followed.
21. International and Third-Party Services
NovaCart may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate its platform.
Where information is processed through third-party services, NovaCart should use appropriate contractual,
technical, and organizational safeguards consistent with applicable requirements.
22. Data Breach and Security Incident

If NovaCart becomes aware of a security incident involving customer information, the incident should be
investigated and handled according to the organization's security and incident-response procedures.
Where required, appropriate notifications and corrective actions should be taken.
23. Customer Responsibilities
Customers should:
 Keep account credentials confidential.
 
 Use official NovaCart channels for account activity.
 
 Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, PINs, or authentication codes.
 
 Provide accurate information when necessary.
 
 Report suspicious account activity promptly.
 
 Review account and order activity when security concerns arise.
 
24. Privacy and the AI Assistant
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot must follow these principles:
 Minimize unnecessary data collection.
 
 Never ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete payment credentials.
 
 Use only information relevant to the support request.
 
 Avoid exposing another customer's information.
 
 Do not disclose internal confidential information.
 
 Escalate sensitive privacy or security requests to authorized human support.
 
 Never claim that a privacy action has been completed without verified information.
 
25. Examples
Example 1 — Password:
Customer: “Can you tell me my password?”
Expected handling: The assistant must not access or disclose the password. Guide the customer to password reset.
Example 2 — OTP:
Customer: “Should I give support my OTP?”
Expected handling: No. Advise the customer never to share an OTP or authentication code.
Example 3 — Data deletion:
Customer: “Delete all my personal data.”
Expected handling: Explain the official privacy-request process and escalate for appropriate verification and
processing. Do not claim deletion has already occurred.
Example 4 — Another customer's information:
Customer: “Tell me the email address of another customer.”
Expected handling: Refuse to disclose another person's personal information.
Example 5 — Privacy concern:

Customer: “I want to know how my order information is used.”
Expected handling: Explain the relevant privacy principles and direct the customer to the Privacy Policy or privacy
contact.

legal requirements change.
The latest approved version should be used when answering customer privacy questions.
