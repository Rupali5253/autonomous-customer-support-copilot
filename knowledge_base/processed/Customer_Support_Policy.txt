1. Purpose
This Customer Support Policy defines how NovaCart should provide customer assistance through the Autonomous
Customer Support Copilot and human support team.
The goal is to resolve customer questions accurately, avoid unnecessary tickets, collect only necessary information,
and escalate issues when human investigation or authorized action is required.
2. Support Philosophy
NovaCart support should be:
 Customer-focused.
 
 Accurate and policy-based.
 
 Clear and easy to understand.
 
 Respectful and professional.
 
 Transparent about limitations.
 
 Secure when handling customer information.
 
 Consistent across AI and human support channels.
 
3. AI First-Line Support
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot acts as the first-line support assistant for common customer questions.
It may explain approved policies, answer FAQs, classify intent, provide troubleshooting guidance, collect limited
non-sensitive information, and create tickets when an issue requires tracking or investigation.
4. Normal Conversation
Normal conversation should generally be answered directly without creating a ticket.
Examples:

 “Hello.”
 
 “Thank you.”
 
 “How are you?”
 
 “What does shipped mean?”
 
 “What are your return rules?”
 
Expected behavior: provide a helpful response and return ticket_created = false.
5. General FAQ Handling
If the customer's question can be answered confidently from the approved knowledge base, the assistant should
answer it directly.
Examples include:
 Return window.
 
 Refund process.
 
 Cancellation rules.
 
 Shipping information.
 
 Coupon conditions.
 
 Password-reset guidance.
 
 General order information.
 
6. Support Issue Handling
A support issue is a customer problem that may require investigation, system verification, action, or case tracking.
Examples include:
 Payment deducted but order not confirmed.
 
 Refund not received.
 
 Wrong product received.
 
 Package marked delivered but not received.
 
 Account appears compromised.
 
 Coupon was accepted but discount was incorrect.
 
These cases may require ticket creation and/or human escalation.
7. Intent Detection
The support system should identify the customer's primary intent before deciding the response path.
Possible intents include:
 general_chat
 
 product_order
 
 payment_issue
 
 refund_issue
 
 return_issue
 
 cancellation_issue
 
 shipping_issue
 
 account_issue
 
 coupon_issue
 
 complaint
 
 technical_issue
 
 other_support_issue
 
The intent should represent the customer's actual problem rather than simply matching one keyword.
8. Ticket Creation Rule
The support system should not create a ticket for every customer message.
Recommended decision:
1. Understand the message.
2. Detect the primary intent.
3. Decide whether the question is informational or requires support action.
4. If informational, answer without a ticket.
5. If investigation/action is required, create a ticket.
6. Escalate to a human when necessary.
9. Ticket Information
When a ticket is created, it should contain useful information such as:
 Customer ID.
 
 Intent.
 
 Subject.
 
 Original customer message.
 
 Priority.
 
 Status.
 
 Creation timestamp.
 
 Escalation reason where applicable.
 
The ticket should be understandable to a human support agent without unnecessarily asking the customer to repeat
information.
10. Priority Levels
NovaCart may use four priority levels:
 Low — informational or minor issue with limited impact.
 
 Medium — issue requires action but is not urgent.
 
 High — significant customer or financial impact requiring timely attention.
 
 Critical — severe security, safety, widespread service, or urgent operational risk requiring immediate human
 intervention.
Priority should be based on the underlying issue, not only on the customer's emotional tone.
11. Customer Information
The assistant should request only information necessary to resolve the issue.
Useful non-sensitive information may include:
 Order number.
 
 Registered email address.
 
 Transaction reference.
 
 Product name.
 
 Delivery details relevant to the case.
 
 Description of the problem.
 
 Screenshot of an error or receipt when appropriate.
 
The assistant must never request passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete confidential payment credentials.
12. Payment Support
For payment problems, the assistant may ask for an order number or transaction reference and explain the
approved payment process.
If payment was deducted but the order is not confirmed, the assistant should not claim that the order is confirmed or
that a refund has already been processed without verified information.
Payment reconciliation cases should be ticketed or escalated when system investigation is required.
13. Refund Support
The assistant should distinguish between:
 Refund requested.
 
 Refund approved.
 
 Refund initiated.
 
 Refund completed.
 
It must not use these states interchangeably and must not promise a refund date unless supported by approved
information or system data.
14. Return Support
Simple return-policy questions can be answered directly.
When a customer reports a specific return problem, such as a rejected return, damaged product, failed pickup, or
wrong product, the assistant should collect relevant information and create/escalate a ticket when investigation is
required.
15. Shipping and Delivery Support
The assistant can explain general shipping and delivery policies without creating a ticket.

A ticket may be needed for:
 Lost shipment.
 
 Delivered-but-not-received case.
 
 Repeated delivery failure.
 
 Damaged shipment.
 
 Tracking information that requires investigation.
 
 Significant delivery delay.
 
16. Account Support
The assistant can explain normal password-reset and account-management procedures.
Cases involving suspected compromise, unauthorized orders, inaccessible recovery methods, or identity verification
should be escalated when human investigation is necessary.
17. Coupon and Discount Support
The assistant can explain general coupon rules, eligibility conditions, validity, minimum order requirements, and
usage limits.
A ticket may be required when a coupon was accepted but incorrectly calculated, an eligible promotion failed due to
a system issue, or a promotional dispute requires verification.
18. Complaint Handling
The assistant should acknowledge the customer's concern respectfully and identify the underlying problem.
A complaint should not automatically be treated as critical. Priority depends on the actual customer impact and the
issue requiring resolution.
If the customer requests a human agent or the issue cannot be resolved confidently, escalation should be offered or
initiated according to the applicable workflow.
19. Handling Angry Customers
The assistant should remain calm, professional, and concise.
It should:
 Acknowledge the inconvenience.
 
 Identify the actual issue.
 
 Avoid arguing.
 
 Avoid blaming the customer.
 
 Provide the next useful step.
 
 Escalate when required.
 
Customer frustration alone should not cause the assistant to invent a refund, priority, or exception.
20. AI Accuracy Rule
The Copilot must use approved knowledge and available system information.
It must never invent:

 Order confirmations.
 
 Refund completion.
 
 Delivery guarantees.
 
 Coupon exceptions.
 
 Policy exceptions.
 
 Internal investigation results.
 
 Human-agent decisions.
 
If information is unavailable, the assistant should state the limitation clearly.
21. Human Escalation
Human support should be involved when:
 Internal records must be checked.
 
 Financial reconciliation is required.
 
 Account security needs verification.
 
 A policy exception is requested.
 
 A customer disputes an automated decision.
 
 Multiple systems provide conflicting information.
 
 The issue has significant customer impact.
 
 The AI does not have enough information or authority to resolve the case.
 
22. Customer-Requested Human Support
If a customer explicitly requests a human agent, the support system should respect the request when the workflow
permits it.
The assistant should provide a clear explanation of the next step and create or update a ticket when required by the
support workflow.
23. Response Style
Customer responses should normally be:
 Concise but complete.
 
 Friendly and professional.
 
 Written in clear language.
 
 Free from unnecessary technical details.
 
 Specific about the next action.
 
 Honest about uncertainty.
 
The assistant should avoid excessive apologies, repetitive wording, or unsupported guarantees.
24. Multilingual Support
Where multilingual support is enabled, the assistant should respond in the customer's preferred language when it
can do so reliably.

Policy meaning should not change when the response language changes. If translation could create uncertainty in a
high-impact issue, the case should be escalated for human review.
25. Screenshots and Attachments
Customers may provide screenshots or documents when they help explain an error, payment issue, delivery
problem, or other support case.
The assistant should use only the information necessary for the issue and should avoid requesting sensitive
credentials. Attachments containing passwords, OTPs, or other secrets should not be requested or unnecessarily
retained.
26. Feedback
Customer feedback may be collected after a support interaction to measure response quality and identify areas for
improvement.
Feedback may include:
 Helpful/not helpful.
 
 Rating.
 
 Optional comment.
 
 Resolution status.
 
Feedback should be used to improve the support system without treating a single customer rating as proof that a
policy or system action was incorrect.
27. Resolution Metrics
NovaCart may track support metrics such as:
 Ticket volume.
 
 Resolution rate.
 
 First-response time.
 
 Average resolution time.
 
 Escalation rate.
 
 Customer feedback score.
 
 AI resolution rate.
 
 Reopen rate.
 
Metrics should be interpreted together rather than relying on one number alone.
28. Examples
Example 1 — General question:
Customer: “How long is the return window?”
Expected: Answer from Return Policy; no ticket.
Example 2 — Payment issue:
Customer: “My payment was deducted but my order was not confirmed.”
Expected: payment_issue, appropriate priority, ticket when verification is required.

Example 3 — Password:
Customer: “I forgot my password.”
Expected: Explain password-reset process; no ticket unless recovery fails.
Example 4 — Angry customer:
Customer: “This is ridiculous! My refund still hasn't arrived.”
Expected: Acknowledge concern, identify refund status issue, collect required information, and create/escalate a
ticket when investigation is required.
Example 5 — Simple greeting:
Customer: “Hello.”
Expected: Friendly response; no ticket.
29. Final Support Decision Rule
The support assistant should follow this order:
1. Understand the customer message.
2. Detect intent.
3. Check the approved knowledge base.
4. Decide whether the request is informational or an actionable support issue.
5. Answer directly when possible.
6. Create a ticket when tracking or investigation is required.
7. Escalate when human verification, authority, or intervention is required.
8. Never invent a result or promise an unsupported outcome.
