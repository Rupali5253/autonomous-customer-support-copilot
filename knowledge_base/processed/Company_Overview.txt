2. About NovaCart
NovaCart is a fictional Indian e-commerce company created for the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot
project. The platform allows customers to browse products, place orders, make secure payments, track deliveries,
request eligible returns or refunds, and receive customer support through digital channels.
NovaCart focuses on providing a simple and reliable online shopping experience while making customer support
faster and more consistent. Its customer-support system combines automated assistance with human escalation
when an issue cannot be safely or accurately resolved by the AI assistant.
3. Business Objective
NovaCart's primary objective is to provide customers with a convenient online shopping experience while
maintaining transparent policies and responsive support. The company aims to reduce unnecessary support wait
times, provide accurate answers to common questions, and ensure that complex or sensitive issues are routed to
human support representatives.
4. Product Categories
NovaCart offers products across several broad categories:
 Electronics & Accessories — phones, computer accessories, audio devices and related electronics.
 Home & Kitchen — appliances, cookware, home organization and household products.
 Fashion & Lifestyle — clothing, footwear, bags and lifestyle accessories.
 Beauty & Personal Care — personal-care products, skincare and grooming products.
 Grocery & Daily Essentials — household consumables, packaged goods and everyday essentials.

5. Customer Ordering Journey
A typical NovaCart customer journey is:
1. Customer creates an account or signs in.
2. Customer browses or searches for products.
3. Customer reviews product information and adds eligible items to the cart.
4. Customer proceeds to checkout and provides delivery details.
5. Customer selects an available payment method.
6. Payment is processed and the order is confirmed after successful verification.
7. NovaCart prepares the order for shipment.
8. The order is handed to the delivery partner.
9. Customer can track the order and receives delivery updates.
10. The order is delivered to the customer.
6. Ordering System
Each confirmed order receives a unique order identifier. Customers can use the order identifier from their account
or confirmation communication when contacting customer support.
Customers may contact support regarding order confirmation, order status, delivery delays, cancellation eligibility,
returns, refunds, damaged products, incorrect products, or payment-related problems. Detailed rules for these
situations are maintained in separate policy documents in the NovaCart knowledge base.
7. Payment Overview
NovaCart supports commonly used payment methods such as credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, selected digital
wallets, and Cash on Delivery where available.
A successful payment should normally result in an order confirmation. If money is deducted but an order is not
confirmed, the customer should retain the transaction details and contact support. Such cases may require
payment verification and can be escalated when the payment cannot be automatically confirmed.
Detailed payment rules, verification procedures, failed-payment handling, and refund timelines are defined in the
Payment Policy and Refund Policy documents.
8. Shipping & Delivery Overview
NovaCart provides delivery services within supported service areas in India. Delivery estimates can vary based on
product availability, seller or fulfillment location, customer location, selected delivery option, and external delivery
conditions.
Customers can use their order information to check shipment and delivery status. Delayed, failed, or incorrectly
delivered orders can be reported to customer support. Detailed delivery timelines, exceptions, tracking rules, and
escalation conditions are maintained in the Shipping & Delivery Policy.
9. Returns, Refunds & Cancellation Overview
NovaCart allows eligible customers to request returns, refunds, or order cancellation according to the applicable
policy and product conditions. Eligibility may depend on product category, order status, shipment status, and the
reason for the request.
Customers should provide accurate order information when contacting support. Detailed eligibility requirements,
time windows, exclusions, refund methods, and processing timelines are defined in the respective Return Policy,
Refund Policy, and Cancellation Policy.
10. Customer Account & Security

Customers are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials. NovaCart provides
account-related support for login problems, password reset, account access, and related issues.
If a customer forgets a password, the customer can use the password-reset process associated with the registered
email address. Security-sensitive cases may require additional verification before support can make account
changes.
11. Customer Support
NovaCart provides customer assistance through website chat, support tickets, and email support. The support
system is designed to distinguish between normal conversations and genuine support issues.
General questions may be answered directly by the AI assistant without creating a support ticket. Issues requiring
investigation, tracking, or human intervention can result in a support ticket. Examples include unresolved payment
problems, certain refund disputes, delivery problems, account-access issues, and other cases requiring
investigation.
12. AI Support & Human Escalation
NovaCart's Autonomous Customer Support Copilot uses intent detection, a company knowledge base,
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and an AI language model to assist customers.
The support flow is:
Customer message → Intent detection → Knowledge retrieval when required → AI response → Resolution or
ticket creation.
If the AI has sufficient information from the approved knowledge base, it can provide a response directly. If the
issue is complex, requires account-specific investigation, involves a policy exception, or cannot be confidently
resolved, the system can create or escalate a support ticket for human review.
13. Support Principles
NovaCart customer support follows these principles:
 Provide clear and professional communication.
 Use approved company knowledge when answering policy-related questions.
 Avoid inventing policy details or unsupported promises.
 Protect customer account and payment information.
 Escalate issues that require human investigation.
 Keep ticket information accurate and traceable.
 Provide customers with relevant next steps whenever possible.
