1. Purpose
This Coupon & Discount Policy defines the rules for NovaCart promotional codes, discounts, vouchers, campaign
offers, eligibility conditions, and common customer questions.
The purpose is to provide customers and the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot with clear and consistent
guidance about how offers can be used.
2. What Is a Coupon?
A coupon is a promotional code or offer that may provide a discount, benefit, or other promotional value when the
applicable conditions are satisfied.
Each coupon may have its own validity period, minimum order value, eligible products, customer eligibility,
payment-method requirements, and usage limits.
3. What Is a Discount?
A discount may be automatically applied to an eligible product, order, category, or campaign without requiring a
coupon code.
The displayed offer terms at checkout should be treated as the primary source for the specific discount.
4. Coupon Eligibility
A coupon may be available only to eligible customers or orders.
Eligibility may depend on:
 Minimum order value.
 
 Specific products or categories.
 
 New or existing customer status.
 
 Selected payment methods.
 
 Geographic availability.
 
 Campaign dates.
 
 Maximum discount limits.
 
 Customer-specific promotional eligibility.
 
5. Coupon Validity
Every promotional coupon should have a defined validity period when applicable.
A coupon normally cannot be used after its expiration date. If an offer displays a specific start and end date, those
dates control the offer's availability.
6. One Coupon Per Order
The standard NovaCart rule is one promotional coupon per order unless a specific campaign explicitly allows
multiple promotional benefits.
Customers should not assume that two coupon codes can be combined merely because both codes are valid
individually.
7. Coupon Stacking
Coupon stacking means applying multiple promotional codes or benefits to the same order.
Unless the offer explicitly states that stacking is permitted, promotional codes should not be combined. Automatic
product discounts and coupons may also have separate combination rules.
8. Minimum Order Value
Some coupons require the cart to reach a minimum order value before the discount can be applied.
The minimum order value is determined by the individual promotion. Shipping charges, taxes, or excluded products
may or may not count toward the threshold depending on the offer terms.
9. Maximum Discount
A coupon may provide a percentage discount subject to a maximum discount amount.
Example: a “20% off up to ■500” coupon provides a percentage discount, but the maximum discount available
under that offer is ■500.
10. Percentage and Fixed-Amount Discounts
NovaCart may offer:
 Percentage discounts such as 10% off.
 
 Fixed-amount discounts such as ■200 off.
 
 Product-specific discounts.
 
 Category-level discounts.
 
 Free-shipping promotions.
 
 Limited-time campaign offers.
 
The individual offer terms determine how the benefit is calculated.
11. Product and Category Restrictions
Some coupons apply only to selected products or categories.

A customer should check the eligible-product conditions before assuming a coupon can be used for the entire cart.
12. Payment Method Restrictions
Certain promotions may require a specific payment method, bank, wallet, card type, or other eligible payment
option.
If the required payment method is not used, the promotion may not apply even when the coupon code itself is valid.
13. New Customer Offers
Some promotional offers may be limited to new customers or first eligible orders.
A customer who has already completed an eligible purchase may not qualify for a first-order promotion. The specific
campaign terms determine eligibility.
14. Coupon Application at Checkout
Customers should enter the coupon code in the designated coupon or promotional-code field and apply it before
completing payment.
The checkout should display whether the coupon was accepted, rejected, or subject to an unmet condition.
15. Invalid Coupon
A coupon may be rejected because:
 It has expired.
 
 It has not started yet.
 
 The order value is too low.
 
 Products are not eligible.
 
 The customer is not eligible.
 
 The coupon has already reached its usage limit.
 
 Another promotion is incompatible.
 
 The code was entered incorrectly.
 
 The required payment method is not selected.
 
The assistant should explain the likely reason without inventing a reason when system information is unavailable.
16. Coupon Code Typing Errors
Customers should verify spelling, spaces, capitalization where applicable, and the complete coupon code.
The assistant may suggest re-entering the code or copying it from the official promotional source.
17. Expired Coupon
An expired coupon normally cannot be applied after the stated validity period.
The assistant should not promise an extension or replacement unless an authorized system or human agent
confirms that an exception is available.
18. Coupon Usage Limits
Promotions may have limits such as:

 One use per customer.
 
 One use per account.
 
 Limited total redemptions.
 
 Limited uses per campaign.
 
 Limited quantity of promotional inventory.
 
Once a published usage limit is reached, the coupon may no longer be available.
19. Fraud and Promotional Abuse
Customers must not misuse coupons by creating accounts, manipulating orders, or using unauthorized methods
solely to obtain promotional benefits.
NovaCart may restrict promotional benefits or investigate suspicious activity where abuse is suspected.
20. Coupon and Cancellation
If an order using a coupon is cancelled, the treatment of the coupon depends on the specific promotion and
applicable cancellation terms.
A cancelled order does not automatically guarantee that the coupon will be restored. The assistant should avoid
promising coupon reactivation without verified policy or system information.
21. Coupon and Refund
When an order containing a promotional discount is refunded, the refund amount should follow the applicable
Refund Policy and the promotional terms.
The assistant should not calculate or guarantee a refund amount when the required order information is unavailable.
22. Coupon and Return
If a customer returns one item from a multi-item order that received a promotional discount, the refund or adjustment
may be affected by the discount structure.
The applicable Return and Refund Policies, along with the individual offer terms, should be used to determine the
correct outcome.
23. Free Shipping Offers
Some promotions may provide free or reduced shipping.
Free-shipping offers may have minimum order values, location restrictions, product restrictions, or validity periods.
The specific offer terms determine eligibility.
24. Personalized Offers
Some discounts may be assigned to a particular customer account and may not be transferable.
A customer should not assume that a personalized coupon can be shared with another account.
25. Price and Discount Display
The checkout should show the applicable discount before order completion whenever the promotion is successfully
applied.

Customers should review the final order summary before completing payment. If the expected discount is missing,
the customer should resolve the issue before placing the order when possible.
26. AI Assistant Rules
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot should:
 Use the approved Coupon & Discount Policy.
 
 Clearly state conditions.
 
 Never invent a coupon code.
 
 Never promise an expired coupon will work.
 
 Never guarantee an exception without authorization.
 
 Distinguish between a valid coupon and an eligible coupon.
 
 Ask for the coupon code or relevant offer details when necessary.
 
 Escalate disputed promotional issues when verification is required.
 
27. When a Ticket Is Needed
A simple question such as “Do you have any coupon rules?” can be answered directly without a ticket.
A ticket may be appropriate when:
 A valid coupon was accepted but the discount was calculated incorrectly.
 
 A promotional discount disappeared after payment.
 
 The customer disputes an offer eligibility decision.
 
 A campaign appears to have been applied incorrectly.
 
 A system issue prevents an eligible coupon from being used.
 
 A manual promotional exception requires authorized review.
 
28. Examples
Example 1 — General question:
Customer: “Can I use a coupon?”
Expected handling: Explain that coupons are subject to their individual terms. No ticket.
Example 2 — Two coupons:
Customer: “Can I use two coupon codes together?”
Expected handling: Explain that the standard rule is one promotional coupon per order unless the specific campaign
permits stacking.
Example 3 — Expired coupon:
Customer: “My coupon expired yesterday. Can you extend it?”
Expected handling: Do not promise an extension. Explain that expired coupons normally cannot be used unless an
authorized exception is confirmed.
Example 4 — Coupon rejected:
Customer: “My coupon isn't working.”

Expected handling: Ask for the coupon code and relevant cart/offer information or explain common eligibility
conditions. Create a ticket if system verification or investigation is required.
Example 5 — Incorrect discount:
Customer: “The coupon was accepted but the discount amount is wrong.”
Expected handling: Treat as a promotional/order issue requiring verification; create a ticket when investigation is
necessary.
