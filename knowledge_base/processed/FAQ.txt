1. Company & General
Q1. What is NovaCart?
A. NovaCart is a fictional e-commerce company created for the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot project. It
provides an online shopping experience with customer accounts, orders, payments, delivery, returns, refunds, and
customer support.
Q2. How can I contact NovaCart support?
A. Customers can contact support through the supported customer-support channels. The project support email is
support@novacart.example.
Q3. What can the AI support assistant help me with?
A. It can answer approved FAQs, explain company policies, classify support issues, provide troubleshooting
guidance, create tickets when required, and escalate complex cases to human support.
Q4. Will every message create a support ticket?
A. No. Greetings, casual conversation, and simple policy questions normally do not create tickets. Tickets are
created when an issue requires tracking, investigation, or action.
2. Account & Password
Q5. I forgot my password. What should I do?
A. Use the official NovaCart password-reset or account-recovery process. The assistant can guide you through the
process.
Q6. Can I give my password to customer support?
A. No. Never share your password with support agents or the AI assistant.
Q7. Should I share an OTP or verification code with support?
A. No. Never share OTPs, PINs, authentication codes, or similar security secrets.

Q8. I see an order I did not place. What should I do?
A. Treat it as a potential account-security issue. Provide the order number or other non-sensitive information and
contact support. A ticket may be created for investigation.
Q9. I received a suspicious login alert. What should I do?
A. Secure your account through the official recovery process and contact support if the activity is not recognized.
Human escalation may be required.
3. Product & Orders
Q10. How do I place an order?
A. Select an eligible product, add it to the cart, provide the required delivery information, select an available payment
method, review the order, and complete checkout.
Q11. Can I change my order after placing it?
A. It depends on the order's fulfillment status. Changes may be possible before fulfillment reaches a restricted
stage. Contact support if the required change cannot be made through the normal order interface.
Q12. Can I cancel my order?
A. Cancellation is generally possible only while the order is in an eligible cancellable stage. Once fulfillment or
shipment reaches a non-cancellable stage, cancellation may not be available.
Q13. How can I check my order status?
A. Use the order-tracking or order-history feature available in the customer account. If the status appears incorrect
or requires investigation, contact support.
Q14. My order details look incorrect. What should I do?
A. Check the order summary first. If the order contains incorrect information that cannot be corrected through the
normal process, contact support and provide the order number.
4. Payment
Q15. What payment methods are supported?
A. Available payment methods are shown during checkout. Payment-method availability may vary by order, location,
or promotional offer.
Q16. My payment failed. What should I do?
A. Verify the selected payment method and try an available alternative if appropriate. If money appears to have
been deducted despite a failed order, contact support for payment reconciliation.
Q17. My payment was deducted but my order was not confirmed. What should I do?
A. This is a payment-support issue. Provide the order number or transaction reference if available. A support ticket
should be created when payment verification or reconciliation is required.
Q18. Can the AI confirm that my payment was successful?
A. Only when verified payment/order information is available. The AI must not assume payment success from the
customer's message alone.
5. Returns
Q19. How many days do I have to return an eligible product?

A. The standard NovaCart return window is 7 calendar days from the date of delivery, unless a specific product
or offer has a different approved return condition.
Q20. Can every product be returned?
A. No. Return eligibility depends on the product and applicable return conditions. Some categories may be
non-returnable.
Q21. I received the wrong product. What should I do?
A. Contact support and provide the order number and relevant product details. A ticket may be created for
investigation.
Q22. I received a damaged product. What should I do?
A. Report the issue promptly and provide the order details and supporting evidence such as a suitable photograph
or screenshot when requested. The case may require human review.
Q23. Can I return a product after the return window?
A. Normally, returns must be requested within the applicable return window unless an approved exception or
product-specific policy applies. The assistant should not promise an exception without authorization.
6. Refunds
Q24. When will I receive my refund?
A. Refund timing depends on the refund process and payment method. The assistant should not promise a specific
date unless verified information supports it.
Q25. What is the difference between refund initiated and refund completed?
A. A refund being initiated means the refund process has started. Refund completed means the transaction has
been successfully processed according to the applicable system status. These statuses should not be treated as
identical.
Q26. My refund has not arrived. What should I do?
A. Provide the relevant order or refund reference and contact support. A ticket may be created if refund investigation
is required.
Q27. Will I always receive the full amount I paid?
A. The final refund amount depends on the applicable Refund Policy, order conditions, discounts, returned items,
shipping charges, and other relevant terms. The assistant should not guarantee an amount without verified order
information.
7. Shipping & Delivery
Q28. How can I track my order?
A. Use the tracking information available through the order-history or tracking feature.
Q29. My order is delayed. What should I do?
A. Check the latest tracking information. If the delay requires investigation or exceeds the applicable delivery
expectations, contact support and a ticket may be created.
Q30. My order says delivered but I did not receive it. What should I do?
A. Contact support promptly and provide the order number. This may require a delivery investigation and human
escalation.

Q31. My package arrived damaged. What should I do?
A. Report the issue to support with the order details and appropriate evidence. The case may require investigation.
Q32. Can the AI guarantee my delivery date?
A. No. The assistant should only provide delivery information supported by approved policy or verified
tracking/system information.
8. Cancellation
Q33. Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?
A. Standard cancellation may not be available after an order reaches a non-cancellable fulfillment or shipment
stage. The applicable Cancellation Policy should be followed.
Q34. I requested cancellation. Is the order definitely cancelled?
A. A cancellation request and a confirmed cancellation are different. The assistant should confirm cancellation only
when verified order information shows that the cancellation is complete.
Q35. What happens to a coupon if I cancel an order?
A. Coupon treatment depends on the specific promotion and cancellation terms. The assistant should not promise
that a coupon will automatically be restored.
9. Coupons & Discounts
Q36. Can I use two coupons on one order?
A. The standard rule is one promotional coupon per order unless a specific campaign explicitly permits stacking.
Q37. Why is my coupon not working?
A. Possible reasons include expiration, minimum order value, product restrictions, customer eligibility, usage limits,
incorrect code entry, or incompatible promotions.
Q38. Can an expired coupon be extended?
A. Not normally. The assistant must not promise an extension unless an authorized exception is confirmed.
Q39. My coupon was accepted but the discount is wrong. What should I do?
A. Provide the order or coupon details. If the discount requires system verification, a support ticket should be
created.
Q40. Can I use a new-customer coupon if I have already ordered?
A. New-customer promotions may be limited to eligible first orders. The individual promotion's terms determine
eligibility.
10. Customer Support & Tickets
Q41. When will a ticket be created?
A. A ticket may be created when the customer has a support issue requiring investigation, action, or tracking.
Q42. Why was no ticket created for my question?
A. Simple questions and general FAQs can often be answered directly, so creating a ticket is unnecessary.
Q43. Can I ask for a human agent?

A. Yes. If the workflow permits, the support system can route the request to human support. Complex or sensitive
cases may also require human escalation.
Q44. What information should I provide to support?
A. Provide only information needed to resolve the issue, such as an order number, registered email address,
transaction reference, product name, or description of the problem.
Q45. What information should I never provide?
A. Never provide passwords, OTPs, PINs, authentication codes, or complete confidential payment credentials to
support.
11. Priority & Escalation
Q46. What is a high-priority issue?
A. High priority may apply to significant financial, order, delivery, security, or customer-impact issues requiring timely
attention.
Q47. What is a critical issue?
A. Critical issues may include severe account-security incidents, widespread service failures, serious safety
concerns, or other situations requiring immediate authorized human intervention.
Q48. Does being angry make my ticket critical?
A. No. Priority is based on the underlying issue and customer impact, not simply the customer's emotional tone.
Q49. When will AI escalate to a human?
A. Escalation may occur when internal verification is required, information is insufficient, a policy exception is
requested, security is involved, systems conflict, or the AI lacks authority to resolve the issue.
12. Privacy & Security
Q50. How does NovaCart use my information?
A. Information may be used to manage accounts, process orders, provide support, handle transactions, improve
services, protect security, and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements.
Q51. Can the AI see another customer's information?
A. It should not disclose or expose another customer's personal information.
Q52. Can I request deletion of my personal information?
A. Privacy requests can be submitted through the applicable privacy process. Some information may need to be
retained for legitimate operational, security, dispute, or legal reasons.
Q53. Where can I ask a privacy question?
A. The project privacy contact is privacy@novacart.example.
13. AI Assistant Behavior
Q54. Can the AI make up an answer if it does not know?
A. No. The AI should use approved knowledge and available system information. If information is unavailable, it
should clearly state the limitation.
Q55. Can the AI promise a refund?

A. No, unless the system has verified the applicable refund status and the assistant is authorized to communicate it.
Q56. Can the AI promise a delivery date?
A. No. It should provide only verified delivery information or approved policy guidance.
Q57. Can the AI create a ticket automatically?
A. Yes, when the detected issue requires tracking or investigation according to the support workflow.
Q58. Will the AI always answer in English?
A. Where multilingual support is enabled, it may respond in the customer's preferred language when it can do so
reliably. The policy meaning should remain unchanged.
14. Troubleshooting & Common Situations
Q59. The website is not working. What should I do?
A. Retry after checking the connection and basic browser conditions. If the issue continues and requires
investigation, contact support.
Q60. I received an error during checkout. What should I do?
A. Note the error message, avoid repeatedly submitting payment if you are unsure whether a transaction was
created, and contact support with the relevant order or transaction information.
Q61. I was charged twice. What should I do?
A. Provide the order or transaction references and contact support. Duplicate-payment cases may require payment
reconciliation.
Q62. I cannot access my account but need my order information.
A. Use the official account-recovery process. If recovery fails, contact support and complete any required
verification.
Q63. My order was cancelled unexpectedly. What should I do?
A. Check the order status and any available cancellation information. If the reason is unclear or disputed, contact
support for investigation.
15. Quick Decision Guide
For the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot:
 Greeting/casual conversation → answer directly → no ticket.
 
 General policy question → retrieve relevant PDF → answer → no ticket.
 
 Specific payment problem → detect payment intent → ticket if verification is required.
 
 Specific refund problem → retrieve Refund Policy → ticket/escalate if investigation is required.
 
 Return eligibility question → retrieve Return Policy → answer if clear.
 
 Wrong/damaged product → support issue → ticket when investigation is required.
 
 Password reset question → retrieve Account & Password Policy → guide customer.
 
 Suspected account compromise → security issue → human escalation as required.
 
 Coupon question → retrieve Coupon & Discount Policy → answer if conditions are clear.
 
 Privacy request → retrieve Privacy Policy → human review when verification/processing is required.
 
16. Important Policy Consistency Rules
The FAQ is a quick-reference document and should not override specialized policies.
When an FAQ answer conflicts with a specialized approved policy, the specialized policy should be retrieved and
treated as authoritative.
For example, the standard return window in this FAQ is 7 calendar days from delivery, but a specific product may
have a different approved return condition. The assistant should retrieve the relevant product-specific or specialized
policy before giving a definitive answer.
