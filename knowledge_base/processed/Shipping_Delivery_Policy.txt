1. Purpose
This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how NovaCart orders are prepared, shipped, tracked, and delivered. It also
provides guidance for common delivery problems such as delays, failed delivery attempts, incorrect addresses,
damaged shipments, and missing packages.
The policy is intended to provide consistent customer guidance and serve as an approved knowledge source for the
Autonomous Customer Support Copilot.
2. Delivery Estimates
NovaCart displays an estimated delivery date or delivery window during checkout and, where available, in the
customer's order details.
Delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless a specific service explicitly provides a guaranteed delivery
commitment. Actual delivery can be affected by inventory processing, logistics conditions, weather, holidays,
operational disruptions, or the customer's location.
3. Order Processing Before Shipment
After an order is placed, NovaCart may perform payment verification, inventory confirmation, order processing,
packing, and shipment preparation.
The order status may progress through stages such as:
 Order placed.
 
 Payment processing or confirmed.
 
 Order confirmed.
 
 Processing or packing.
 
 Shipped.
 
 Out for delivery.
 
 Delivered.
 
Customers should use the latest order status as the primary indicator of the current fulfillment stage.
4. Shipping and Dispatch
An order is considered shipped when it has been handed over to the designated logistics or delivery partner and a
shipment record is created.
After shipment, tracking information may become available. There can be a short delay between physical handover
and the appearance of the first tracking event.
The AI assistant should not claim that an order has shipped unless the order system or available tracking
information confirms the shipment status.
5. Order Tracking
Eligible shipped orders may include a tracking reference and tracking status.
Customers can generally check tracking through their account or the order details page. Common tracking states
may include:
 Shipment created.
 
 Picked up.
 
 In transit.
 
 Arrived at delivery facility.
 
 Out for delivery.
 
 Delivery attempted.
 
 Delivered.
 
Tracking events may not update continuously. A period without a new scan does not automatically mean that a
package is lost.
6. Delayed Delivery
A delivery is considered delayed when it has not arrived within the applicable estimated delivery window or when
tracking indicates an unexpected logistics delay.
Possible causes include:
 Transportation disruption.
 
 Weather or local conditions.
 
 High shipment volume.
 
 Address or access issues.
 
 Delivery-partner operational delays.
 
 Inventory or dispatch delays.
 
 Other unforeseen events.
 
Support should review the latest order and tracking status before giving a customer a specific explanation.
7. Delivery Date Passed
If the estimated delivery date has passed, the customer should first check the latest tracking status.

If tracking shows that the shipment is still moving, support may provide the latest available information and explain
that the delivery is delayed.
If there is no meaningful tracking progress or the shipment appears stuck, the issue may require a delivery
investigation and human escalation.
8. Failed Delivery Attempt
A delivery attempt may fail because the customer was unavailable, the address could not be located, access was
restricted, the phone number could not be reached, or another delivery condition prevented completion.
Where supported, another delivery attempt may be scheduled. Customers should ensure that the delivery address
and contact details are correct and that someone is available when required.
9. Incorrect or Incomplete Address
Customers should verify the delivery address before placing an order.
If an address is incorrect after an order is placed, the ability to change it depends on the order's current fulfillment
stage. Address changes may not be possible after shipment.
The AI assistant should not promise an address change without checking the current order status and supported
process.
10. Delivery to the Wrong Address
If a customer believes a package was delivered to an incorrect location, support should verify the delivery record
and available proof of delivery.
The customer may be asked for the order number and relevant delivery details. The issue may require investigation
by the delivery partner or NovaCart operations team.
The AI assistant should not claim that a package is lost or delivered incorrectly without evidence.
11. Package Marked Delivered but Not Received
If tracking shows “delivered” but the customer cannot locate the package, the customer should first check whether:
 Another household member received it.
 
 A building reception or security desk accepted it.
 
 The delivery partner left it at an approved location.
 
 The delivery record contains additional information.
 
If the package still cannot be located, the customer should contact support for a delivery investigation. A ticket may
be created when delivery verification is required.
12. Damaged Shipment
Customers should report shipping damage as soon as reasonably possible.
Support may request:
 Order number.
 
 Description of the damage.
 
 Photographs of the package and product.
 
 Delivery information.
 
Customers should retain the packaging and product until support provides further instructions. Depending on the
circumstances, the case may be handled under the Return Policy or Refund Policy.
13. Lost Shipment
A shipment may require investigation when tracking indicates that it has been lost, remains inactive for an unusually
long period, or the delivery partner cannot confirm its location.
Support should verify the tracking history before classifying a package as lost. A confirmed lost-shipment case may
require human escalation and coordination with the delivery partner.
The AI assistant should not promise a refund or replacement before the applicable investigation is completed.
14. Delivery Attempt and Customer Availability
Customers should ensure that someone is available to receive deliveries when required.
If a delivery attempt fails because the recipient is unavailable, another attempt may be made where the delivery
service supports it. Repeated failed attempts may result in the shipment being returned to the sender.
Customers should follow any instructions shown in the tracking information or delivery communication.
15. Delivery Restrictions
Certain products, addresses, buildings, or locations may have delivery restrictions.
Delivery availability can depend on:
 Product category.
 
 Delivery location.
 
 Logistics coverage.
 
 Local restrictions.
 
 Operational conditions.
 
 Seller or fulfillment requirements.
 
The delivery options displayed during checkout should be treated as the current availability for the order.
16. Delivery Charges
Any applicable delivery or shipping charge should be displayed during checkout before the customer confirms the
order.
Delivery charges may vary based on product, order value, delivery location, fulfillment method, promotions, or other
applicable conditions.
The AI assistant should not invent a delivery fee when the actual order information is not available.
17. Delivery and Cancellation
Once an order enters shipment or a non-cancellable fulfillment stage, standard cancellation may no longer be
available.
Customers who no longer want a shipped order may need to use the applicable return process after delivery,
provided the product is eligible.
The Cancellation Policy defines cancellation rules, while this document covers shipment and delivery handling.

18. Delivery and Returns
If a delivered product is damaged, incorrect, defective, or otherwise eligible for return, the customer may use the
Return Policy.
Delivery-related evidence such as photographs, tracking information, and delivery records may be relevant to the
return investigation.
The AI assistant should connect the customer to the appropriate return process instead of creating an unrelated
delivery resolution.
19. Delivery Ticket and Escalation
A general question such as “Where can I track my order?” can normally be answered without creating a ticket.
A ticket or human escalation may be appropriate when:
 A package is marked delivered but cannot be located.
 
 Tracking has stopped progressing and investigation is required.
 
 A shipment is suspected to be lost.
 
 A delivery was made to the wrong location.
 
 A damaged shipment requires verification.
 
 Repeated delivery attempts have failed.
 
 The order or tracking information is inconsistent.
 
20. Information Required for Delivery Support
Support may request non-sensitive information such as:
 Order number.
 
 Tracking number, if available.
 
 Delivery address confirmation at a non-sensitive level.
 
 Description of the delivery issue.
 
 Approximate expected delivery date.
 
 Photographs for damage-related cases.
 
Customers should not provide passwords, OTPs, PINs, or confidential payment credentials.
21. Examples
Example 1 — Tracking question:
Customer: “Where is my order?”
Expected handling: Ask for or use the order details, provide the latest available tracking status, and avoid inventing
a delivery location.
Example 2 — Delayed order:
Customer: “My order was supposed to arrive yesterday.”
Expected handling: Check the latest status, explain that the shipment is delayed if supported by tracking, and
escalate if the shipment requires investigation.
Example 3 — Delivered but missing:

Customer: “Tracking says delivered but I don't have the package.”
Expected handling: Ask the customer to check common delivery locations and then create/escalate a delivery
investigation if the package remains missing.
Example 4 — Wrong address:
Customer: “I entered the wrong address.”
Expected handling: Check the current fulfillment stage. If an address change is still supported, guide the customer;
otherwise explain the available next step without promising a change.

knowledge base.
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot should use the current order and tracking information together with this
