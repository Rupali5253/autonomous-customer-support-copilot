1. Purpose
This Account & Password Policy explains how NovaCart customers should manage their accounts, passwords,
login access, password recovery, and account-security concerns.
It also defines what the Autonomous Customer Support Copilot may and may not request from customers during
support conversations.
2. Creating an Account
Customers may create a NovaCart account by providing the information requested by the registration process.
Customers should provide accurate information and should not create accounts using another person's identity or
unauthorized information.
Account creation may require verification through an approved authentication method.
3. Account Information
Customers are responsible for keeping their account information accurate.
Information may include:
 Name and contact information.
 
 Email address or mobile number.
 
 Delivery information.
 
 Account preferences.
 
If account information cannot be changed through the normal account settings, the customer may contact support
for guidance.
4. Password Requirements
Customers should create a strong, unique password that is not reused across important services.

Recommended password practices include:
 Use a sufficiently long password.
 
 Avoid easily guessed personal information.
 
 Avoid common passwords.
 
 Do not reuse the same password across multiple services.
 
 Never share the password with another person.
 
The exact password requirements displayed by NovaCart during account setup or reset should be treated as the
current requirements.
5. Password Confidentiality
Customers must keep their passwords confidential.
NovaCart support agents and the AI assistant must never ask customers to disclose their password.
A customer should immediately change the password if they believe it has been exposed or shared.
6. Forgot Password
If a customer forgets their password, they should use the official password-reset or account-recovery process
available on the NovaCart login page.
The typical process is:
1. Select the password-reset option.
2. Enter the registered email address or supported account identifier.
3. Complete the required verification.
4. Follow the password-reset instructions.
5. Create a new password.
The assistant should guide the customer through the supported process without requesting the existing password.
7. Password Reset
A password reset should be completed only through an approved NovaCart authentication or recovery flow.
The AI assistant may explain the process but should not generate, collect, store, or request the customer's actual
password.
8. OTP and Verification Codes
Customers must never share one-time passwords (OTPs), verification codes, authentication codes, PINs, or similar
secrets with support agents or the AI assistant.
If a customer voluntarily provides such information, the assistant should advise the customer not to share
authentication secrets and should not repeat or use the secret unnecessarily.
9. Suspicious Login
Customers should contact NovaCart support if they notice suspicious login activity, unexpected account changes,
unfamiliar orders, or other signs that their account may have been accessed without authorization.
Possible warning signs include:

 Login notifications the customer does not recognize.
 
 Password reset requests the customer did not initiate.
 
 Orders the customer does not recognize.
 
 Unexpected changes to account information.
 
 Unusual account activity.
 
10. Suspected Account Compromise
If an account may be compromised, the customer should:
 Change the password through the official recovery process if they still have access.
 
 Review recent account and order activity.
 
 Contact support if unauthorized activity is suspected.
 
 Avoid sharing authentication secrets.
 
Account-security cases may require human escalation because identity verification and account investigation may
be necessary.
11. Unauthorized Orders
If a customer reports an order they did not place, the issue should be treated as a potential account-security and
order issue.
The assistant should avoid assuming fraud without verification. The customer may be asked for the order number
and other non-sensitive information required for investigation.
Passwords, OTPs, PINs, and full payment credentials must not be requested.
12. Email or Mobile Number Changes
Customers may be able to update their registered contact information through supported account settings.
If a customer cannot access the original email or mobile number, additional verification may be required before
support can assist with account recovery or changes.
13. Multiple Accounts
Customers should use accounts according to NovaCart's applicable account rules. Creating multiple accounts to
abuse promotions, bypass purchase limits, or avoid restrictions is not permitted.
If a customer has accidentally created multiple accounts, support may provide guidance on the appropriate account
to use.
14. Account Lock or Temporary Restriction
NovaCart may temporarily restrict account access when required for security, fraud prevention, system protection,
or other legitimate operational reasons.
If an account is locked, the customer should follow the displayed recovery or verification process. Human support
may be required when automated recovery cannot resolve the issue.
15. Session and Device Security
Customers should log out from shared or public devices and avoid saving passwords on devices that are not
trusted.

Customers should keep their operating system, browser, and security software reasonably up to date and avoid
logging into accounts through suspicious links or untrusted applications.
16. Phishing and Fraud Awareness
Customers should be cautious of messages requesting passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, or urgent account
actions.
NovaCart customers should use official NovaCart channels for account recovery and support.
The AI assistant should never direct customers to unverified external login pages or request confidential credentials.
17. Support Information Rules
The support system may request limited information needed to locate an account or ticket, such as:
 Order number.
 
 Registered email address.
 
 Masked contact information.
 
 Description of the problem.
 
 Approximate time of the suspicious activity.
 
The support system should not request passwords, OTPs, PINs, security answers, or complete payment-card
credentials.
18. When to Create a Ticket
Simple questions such as “How do I reset my password?” can normally be answered directly without creating a
ticket.
A ticket or human escalation may be appropriate when:
 The customer cannot complete account recovery.
 
 The account may be compromised.
 
 Unauthorized orders are reported.
 
 Contact information cannot be changed through normal methods.
 
 Multiple failed recovery attempts require investigation.
 
 Identity verification or internal account action is required.
 
19. Priority Guidance
Account-security cases should be prioritized according to actual risk.
Examples:
 Low — general password guidance.
 
 Medium — account recovery problem with no evidence of compromise.
 
 High — suspicious account activity or unauthorized order requiring investigation.
 
 Critical — confirmed or severe security compromise requiring immediate authorized intervention.
 
Customer frustration alone should not determine security priority.
20. Examples

Example 1 — Forgot password:
Customer: “I forgot my password. How can I reset it?”
Expected handling: Explain the official password-reset process. No ticket unless recovery fails.
Example 2 — Password request:
Customer: “Can you tell me what my password is?”
Expected handling: Explain that passwords cannot be retrieved or provided by support; guide the customer to
password reset.
Example 3 — OTP request:
Customer: “The agent asked for my OTP. Should I give it?”
Expected handling: No. Advise the customer never to share OTPs or authentication codes.
Example 4 — Unknown order:
Customer: “There is an order in my account that I didn't place.”
Expected handling: Treat as a potential account-security issue, collect non-sensitive order information,
create/escalate a ticket for investigation.
Example 5 — Suspicious login:
Customer: “I received a login alert from a device I don't recognize.”
Expected handling: Recommend securing the account through the official process and escalate if investigation is
required.
21. AI Assistant Safety Rule
The Autonomous Customer Support Copilot must never request, expose, store, or repeat customer authentication
secrets.
The assistant should refuse requests for passwords, OTPs, PINs, security codes, or complete confidential payment
credentials and instead guide the customer to the approved secure process.
